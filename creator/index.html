FAQ

Questions brands ask

Do you do barter collabs?

No. Every collab is paid. You're welcome to send the product too, so I can try it properly before I film.

Can we write the script?

Send me your key points and the offer. I write the hook and film it in my own style, because that's what my audience watches to the end. You approve the concept and the final cut.

Can we run the video as an ad?

Yes. Book the UGC package, or add ad rights to any Reel. You get the full-quality file.

How fast can it go live?

Usually six days from the brief. Launch-day store visits can be booked at shorter notice.

How do I know it worked?

Seven days after posting you get a report with views, watch time, link clicks and code uses. Give me your own discount code, so you can check the sales on your side too.
