The residence

A house built to disappear into weather.

Casa Horizonte sits forty metres above the water on a basalt shelf that has been losing its argument with the Atlantic for eleven million years. The house does not fight it. Three poured concrete fins anchor into the rock, and everything above them is glass.

The living floor cantilevers four metres past the cliff edge. On still mornings the horizon reads as a single unbroken line across twenty-two metres of glazing. In an Atlantic storm, which arrives roughly nine times a winter, the whole western elevation becomes a wall of moving water and the house stays perfectly dry and perfectly quiet.

The place

Twenty-five minutes from Lisbon, and nowhere near it.

Azenhas do Mar is a village of white houses stacked down a cliff, with one restaurant that matters and a tidal pool that fills twice a day. Sintra and its forests are eleven minutes inland. Lisbon airport is forty minutes by road.
