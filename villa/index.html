Questions

Can a non-resident buy this?

Yes. Arabian Ranches is a freehold area, so any nationality may buy outright, resident or not. You do not need a residence visa to own, and ownership of a property at this value can itself support a long-term visa application.

How long does transfer take?

For a cash purchase, usually two to three weeks from signed contract to title deed. With a mortgage, allow four to six weeks, most of which is the lender's valuation and approval.

What is the service charge for?

Community maintenance: the gates and security, landscaping of the public areas, the shared pool and parks, and road upkeep. It is billed annually and is separate from your own utilities.
