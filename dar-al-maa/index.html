Straight answers

What buyers ask us first.

Is it really still for sale?

Yes. Before listing it we checked the title deed and the seller's ID with the Dubai Land Department. You see both at the viewing, before any offer.

Do I pay anything to view it?

No, never. Nobody from us will ask for a deposit or a booking fee to see the house. If anyone does, it is not us.

Can I buy it if I am not a UAE national?

Yes. Palm Jumeirah is freehold for every nationality, and the title deed goes in your own name.

Does it come with a Golden Visa?

Property worth AED 2 million or more qualifies the owner to apply for the 10 year UAE Golden Visa. This house clears that many times over, and we can introduce you to a firm that handles the application.

What will it cost to run each year?

The service charge is about AED 62,000 a year. Add DEWA for power and water, the municipality housing fee on your DEWA bill, and pool and garden care.
